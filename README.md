# DevNote

**DevNote** is a simple and efficient note-taking application designed to help developers and software engineers organize their thoughts, track their progress, and store important information related to their work. Whether you are writing code, taking project notes, or just keeping track of tasks, DevNote provides a lightweight and accessible solution for all your note-taking needs.

## Features

- **Markdown Support**: Write and format your notes using Markdown syntax.
- **Categorization**: Organize your notes by categories for better management.
- **Search**: Quickly search for specific notes.
- **Tagging**: Add custom tags to your notes for easy filtering.
- **Local Storage**: All your notes are saved locally on your device for privacy and security.
- **Simple UI**: An intuitive user interface for seamless note-taking experience.
